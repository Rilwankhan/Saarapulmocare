About Us

Compassionate Care for Every Breath

At Sara Pulmocare, we believe healthy breathing is the foundation of a fulfilling life. Our clinic in Thillai Nagar, Trichy is dedicated to delivering world-class respiratory care through advanced diagnostics and personalized treatment plans.

Led by Dr. Raj Thilak — an MD with over a decade of specialized experience in pulmonology — our team combines clinical expertise with genuine compassion to guide patients from diagnosis to complete recovery.

  • State-of-the-art spirometry & lung function testing
  • Evidence-based protocols for asthma, COPD, and ILD
  • Personalized care plans tailored to each patient
  • Patient education for long-term respiratory wellness
  • Warm, friendly clinic environment in Thillai Nagar

Wheezing Treatment

Asthma Care

Allergy Testing & Management

Chronic Cough Treatment
